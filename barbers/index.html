Questions barbers ask

What does it cost?

It depends on your shop and what you need, so I quote you on the call. The call is free and there's no obligation.

Will it make up prices or promises?

It only answers from the fact sheet I write with you, and it's told never to make things up. When it doesn't know something, it tells the client to call or text you. It can even say a promo has ended, so nobody shows up expecting an old deal.

Can it book appointments for me?

Not by itself, and that's on purpose. It sends clients to the booking page you already use, so your schedule never gets touched by a bot.

Do I need a new website?

No. It's added to the website you have. If you don't have one yet, tell me on the call and we'll figure out the simplest option.

What happens when my prices change?

Tell me and I update the fact sheet. The receptionist is only as right as what it's given, so keeping it current is part of the deal.

Is my information safe?

The AI service's key lives on the server, never in your website's code, so nobody can copy it and run up a bill. The receptionist only knows what's on your fact sheet: public info like prices and hours.

Will it sound like a robot?

It's set up to answer like a front desk, short and friendly, the way you'd text a client. Try the one on my shop's site: tap the chat bubble and ask it something.
