About

It started with my own shop. I needed a website for T3Trims, so I built one: a booking site with an AI chat receptionist that answers clients' questions about prices, hours and styles at any time of day. I'm at the chair from noon to eight, so anything that saves me a phone call is worth building.

Since then I've shipped more than ten tools. I build with AI, so I go from idea to working product in days, and I spend my time on what matters: what the tool should actually do for the person using it.

Running a small business taught me what small businesses need: fewer missed bookings, less admin, and tools that work without a manual. That's what I build now.
